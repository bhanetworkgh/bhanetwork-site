# Photos

Real photos from the team, for the large photo areas on the site (the hero, the
stats band, the about block, the principles split, the footer and the circle cluster).

1. Put the file in this folder (JPG or PNG, landscape, at least 2400 px wide where you can).
2. Name it in `manifest.json` under its slot with an `alt` line saying what is in it.
3. Commit. The build turns it into WebP in four widths.

A photo of the vFarm rig itself does not go here: only the approved renders in
`public/renders/` may show the rig.
